THE SEA EATER — OFFICIAL SPECIFICATIONS & SCALE

THE SEA EATER

STAGGERINGLY MASSIVE LEVIATHAN

Created by horror artist Trevor Henderson. The Sea Eater is a cosmic-scale ocean terror resembling a titanic moving mountain churning through the world's deepest abysses.

  • Height: ~5 km (3 miles) tall
  • Width: ~4.5 km (4 miles) wide
  • Length: ≥ 25 km (15 miles) long — resembling a living mountain
  • Hide & Biology: Thick blubbery black and cyan hide with continuous hydro-channels letting ocean water flow straight through, giving it astounding hydrodynamic agility.
  • Habitat: Roams the deep abyss of the Pacific and Indian Oceans.

THE 5 KM BONE ARMS

AMALGAMATION OF SLAIN GIANTS

Its two giant arms extend over 5 kilometers into the sky. They are composed entirely of dead marine animals (megalodons, blue whales, colossal squids) and the calcified skeletal remains of defeated ocean giants.

  • Limbs: 2 colossal arms, each > 5,000 meters in length
  • Hands: Humanoid bone hands with abnormal amounts of stretchy skin webbing between elongated fingers
  • Grip: Capable of trapping supertankers, battleships, or entire fleets like crumbs
  • Auditory Signature: Iconic subterranean roar accompanied by bone clattering and crushing vortex whirlpools

CONTAINER VESSEL

HUMAN CIVILIAN SHIP

A 200-meter commercial freight ship caught in the violent tempest generated by the beast's displacement. Swallowed entirely in a single gulp.

  • Vessel Length: 200 meters (~0.8% of the Sea Eater's length)
  • Tonnage: 120,000 DWT
  • Fate: Pulled into the abyssal maw; zero survivors
