# The Sea Eater (Trevor Henderson) — 30-Second Oceanic Horror Scene

An interactive, cinematic Web Audio and procedural HTML5 Canvas animation depicting **The Sea Eater**, the gargantuan cosmic titan from the horror mythos of **Trevor Henderson**.

![The Sea Eater](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Danielwu9226/OMA-Animation/main/screenshot.png)

---

## 🌊 Overview & Lore

- **Creature**: The Sea Eater (Trevor Henderson Mythos)
- **Scale**: ~5 km (3 miles) tall, ~4.5 km (4 miles) wide, and &ge; 25 km (15 miles) long resembling an undulating mountain.
- **Anatomy**:
  - Central colossal mountain dome with a towering, pitch-black arched cavern mouth / tunnel.
  - Two giant bent stilt limbs with acute high elbow joints reaching 5 km into the sky and flaring out as they pierce the ocean surface.
  - Pale, murky bone-grey and olive-tinted blubbery hide.
- **Habitat**: Pacific and Indian Ocean abyssal deep trenches (Mariana Trench, 10,920m depth).

---

## ⏱️ 30-Second Scene Choreography

1. **00s – 06s | Act 1: The Mountain Awakens**:
   The colossal 25 km central arch mound surfaces from the Pacific depths in a foggy twilight tempest, with tiny flocks of birds circling the 5 km high joints.
2. **06s – 13s | Act 2: 5km Stilt Limbs**:
   The titanic bent limbs heave and step through the ocean swells, sending massive waves and foam wakes outward.
3. **13s – 20s | Act 3: The Iconic Roar**:
   The dark arched cavern mouth trembles and releases the iconic world-shattering infrasound roar; sonic shockwaves burst across the sea and lightning silhouettes the limbs.
4. **20s – 25s | Act 4: Ship Sighted**:
   A 200m cargo vessel is caught in the tempest right in front of the giant archway tunnel. The ship sounds its desperate distress foghorn.
5. **25s – 30s | Act 5: Swallowed Whole**:
   A catastrophic whirlpool vortex forms at the arched tunnel entrance, sucking the ship straight into the endless dark cavern of the Sea Eater before the titan sinks back into the abyss.

---

## 🎮 Features & Controls

- **Spacebar**: Play / Pause
- **R**: Restart from 00:00
- **Timeline Scrubber**: Click & drag anywhere along the 30-second timeline
- **Scene Chips**: Jump directly to Acts 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5
- **Iconic Roar**: Manually trigger the roar and shockwave effect at any time
- **Camera Switcher**: Toggle between `Cinematic Cam`, `Ship POV Cam`, and `Aerial Titan Cam`
- **VHS Found-Footage Filter**: Toggle vintage VHS analog tape grain and scanline overlay
- **Lore Specs Modal**: View full mythological specifications and accurate scale comparison diagram (Human vs Whale vs Ship vs Burj Khalifa vs Everest vs Sea Eater)
