# The Sea Eater vs The Bloop — 1-Minute Oceanic Clash (60-Second Fight Scene)

An interactive, cinematic Web Audio and procedural HTML5 Canvas animation depicting the apocalyptic duel between **The Sea Eater** (the cosmic titan from the horror mythos of **Trevor Henderson**) and **The Bloop** (the pale leviathan whale-like deep-sea cryptid).

---

## 🌊 Overview & Combatant Lore

### 1. The Sea Eater (Trevor Henderson Mythos)
- **Scale**: ~5 km (3 miles) tall, ~4.5 km (4 miles) wide, &ge; 25 km (15 miles) long resembling a moving mountain.
- **Anatomy**:
  - Central colossal mountain dome with a towering, pitch-black arched cavern mouth / tunnel.
  - Two giant bent stilt limbs with acute high elbow joints reaching 5 km into the misty sky and flaring out with webbed humanoid bone claws as they pierce the ocean surface.
  - Thick blubbery bone-grey and olive-tinted hide with continuous hydro-channels and cascading waterfalls.
- **Habitat**: Mariana Trench abyssal deeps (10,920m depth).

### 2. The Bloop (Deep Sea Whale Leviathan)
- **Scale**: Up to 12 school buses long (~144–155 meters).
- **Anatomy**:
  - Pale gray/ice-blue whale-like muscular body with distinct segmented neck plates and wrinkly ventral grooves.
  - Massive round soulless black eyes with glistening reflections.
  - **Giant unhinging snake jaw**: Opens to an astonishing 140° gape, exposing an endless pitch-black circular gullet lined with hundreds of razor-sharp needle teeth.
  - Powerful pectoral flippers and a massive horizontal whale tail fluke capable of detonating 100m tsunami walls.
- **Acoustic Signature**: 1997 NOAA ultralow-frequency infrasonic marine recording (frequency chirp rising from 10Hz to 40Hz) paired with a terrifying biological whale screech.

## ⏱️ 60-Second Fight Choreography (All 6 Combat Moves)

1. **00s – 08s | Act 1: Abyssal Contact & Sonar Awakening**:
   The colossal 25 km Sea Eater emerges in a foggy twilight tempest. The Bloop surfaces from the deep right flank (12 school buses long), soulless black eyes locking onto the mountain titan as hydrophones spike.
2. **08s – 17s | Act 2: [MOVE] Bloop Torpedo Charge & Sea Eater Stilt Dodge**:
   The Bloop accelerates into a 85-knot hydrodynamic torpedo charge with cavitation foam wakes. The Sea Eater heaves its 5 km bent stilt limb into the clouds, deftly dodging the charge as the Bloop crashes through the sea beneath the archway.
3. **17s – 26s | Act 3: [MOVE] Sea Eater 5km Claw Swipe & Bloop Dive Dodge**:
   The Sea Eater brings its 5 km limb crashing down in a supersonic webbed claw swipe, carving glowing bioluminescent water spray. The Bloop performs an emergency dive dodge beneath the swells, narrowly avoiding the lethal talons.
4. **26s – 35s | Act 4: [MOVE] Bloop Unhinged Snake Jaw Bite**:
   The Bloop explodes out of the ocean, unhinging its snake jaws to a 140° gape and clamping its needle-tooth maw onto the Sea Eater's stilt limb! Severe screen shake, titan ichor erupts, and the Sea Eater releases its iconic shuddering infrasound roar in fury.
5. **35s – 43s | Act 5: [MOVE] Bloop Whale Tail Smack Tsunami**:
   Using the momentum of its roll, the Bloop whips its colossal whale fluke down onto the ocean surface, detonating a 100-meter tsunami wave wall that slams violently into the Sea Eater's mountain flank.
6. **43s – 52s | Act 6: [MOVE] Sea Eater Mountain Ramming Charge**:
   Enraged, the Sea Eater surges forward like a moving continent, ramming the Bloop with apocalyptic tectonic mass and blinding bow waves, knocking the whale leviathan backward into the churning foam.
7. **52s – 60s | Act 7: [MOVE] Arched Maw Bite & Dual Roar Finale**:
   The Sea Eater's central arched cavernous maw opens wide with crushing vortex suction, lunging down to snap at the Bloop as both titans duel with clashing jaws and roars into the foggy tempest!

## 🎮 Features & Interactive Controls

- **Spacebar**: Play / Pause
- **R**: Restart from 00:00.00
- **N**: Jump to Next Fight Act
- **E**: Trigger Sea Eater's Iconic Infrasound Roar
- **B**: Trigger The Bloop's Infrasonic Sonar Scream
- **M**: Mute / Unmute Audio
- **C**: Switch Camera View (`Cinematic Cam`, `Sea Eater POV`, `The Bloop Cam`, `Abyss Depth Cam`, `Aerial Titan Cam`)
- **V**: Toggle Analog VHS Found-Footage Filter
- **Export Video Button**: One-click record and download of the complete 1-minute battle as a high-definition `.webm` video!
- **Interactive Scrubber**: Click and drag anywhere along the 60-second timeline.
- **Quick Act Chips**: Jump straight to any of the 7 acts and combat moves.
- **Playback Speed**: 0.5x, 1.0x, 1.5x, 2.0x.
- **Combatant Telemetry**: Real-time HUD tracking health/energy meters, active moves, impact force (Giganewtons), seismograph frequency (Hz), and distance between beasts.
