# The Sea Eater vs The Bloop — 3D Oceanic Fight Scene to the Death (60 Seconds)

## ⏱️ 60-Second Fight Choreography (Fight to the Death)

1. **00s – 08s | Act 1: Abyssal Awakening & Sonar Contact**:
   The colossal 25 km Sea Eater heaves out of Mariana trench fog. The Bloop breaches at 40 knots on the right flank. Authentic 1997 NOAA infrasound chirp (10Hz–40Hz) and shuddering eldritch mountain roars echo across the 3D ocean.
2. **08s – 17s | Act 2: [MOVE] Bloop Torpedo Charge & Sea Eater Stilt Dodge**:
   The Bloop surges into a 90-knot hydrodynamic charge with foaming cavitation wakes. The Sea Eater heaves its 5 km bent stilt limb high into the storm clouds, dodging as the Bloop crashes beneath the archway.
3. **17s – 26s | Act 3: [MOVE] Sea Eater 5km Claw Swipe & Bloop Dive Dodge**:
   The Sea Eater brings its 5 km limb crashing down in a supersonic webbed claw swipe, carving glowing cyan shockwaves. The Bloop executes an emergency dive beneath the swells, narrowly avoiding lethal talons.
4. **26s – 35s | Act 4: [MOVE] Bloop 140° Snake Jaw Bite & Titan Ichor**:
   The Bloop explodes vertically out of the sea, unhinging its snake jaw to a 140° gape and clamping needle teeth onto the Sea Eater's stilt limb! Severe screen shake, neon-emerald titan ichor erupts, and the Sea Eater screams in fury.
5. **35s – 43s | Act 5: [MOVE] Bloop Whale Fluke Tsunami Detonation**:
   The Bloop whips its colossal whale fluke down, detonating a 100-meter tsunami wave wall that crashes violently into the Sea Eater's mountain flank.
6. **43s – 52s | Act 6: [MOVE] Sea Eater Mountain Ramming Charge**:
   Enraged, the Sea Eater surges forward like a moving continent, ramming 8.4&times;10<sup>11</sup> tons of mass into the Bloop, cracking bone and knocking the leviathan back into churning foam.
7. **52s – 60s | Act 7: [FATAL CLIMAX] Mutual Death Strike & Abyssal Extinction**:
   Mortally wounded, the Bloop executes a suicidal 140° throat lunge into the Sea Eater's central core; simultaneously, the Sea Eater's arched cavernous maw snaps down with crushing guillotine tectonic force, severing the Bloop's spine. Both ancient titans, locked in fatal agony, collapse into the boiling 11,000m Mariana Trench void! Both health meters flatline to 0% — **Mutual Fatal Casualty**.

## 🎮 Features & Interactive Controls

- **True 3D WebGL Engine (Three.js)**: Real-time dynamic lighting, stormy lightning strikes, undulating procedural ocean swells, rain particles, titan ichor bursts, and volumetric depth fog.
- **Sketchfab 3D Models Inspector Modal**: Click **"Sketchfab 3D Models"** in the top bar to inspect both original 3D models live in embedded 360° interactive viewers side-by-side with full metadata and creator attribution.
- **6 3D Camera Modes**:
  - `Cinematic Cam`: Dynamic director's cut tracking combat beats and close-up impacts.
  - `Sea Eater POV`: 1st-person camera mounted 5 km up on the titan's head looking down.
  - `The Bloop Cam`: 3rd-person chase camera right behind the Bloop's dorsal spine.
  - `Abyss Depth Cam`: Deep underwater perspective looking up at silhouetted titans.
  - `Aerial Titan Cam`: High-altitude satellite view.
  - `Free Orbit 3D Cam`: Full user mouse/touch rotation and zoom using OrbitControls!
- **60-Second Video Exporter**: Click **"Export Video (60s)"** to record the real-time 3D canvas and procedural Web Audio into a high-definition `.webm` video file downloaded automatically upon completion.
- **Spacebar**: Play / Pause
- **R**: Restart from 00:00.00
- **N**: Jump to Next Fight Act
- **E**: Trigger Sea Eater's Iconic Infrasound Roar
- **B**: Trigger The Bloop's Infrasonic Sonar Scream
- **M**: Mute / Unmute Audio
- **C**: Switch Camera View
- **V**: Toggle Analog VHS Found-Footage Filter
- **Interactive Scrubber**: Drag anywhere along the 60-second timeline.
- **Combatant Telemetry**: Real-time HUD tracking health/energy meters, active moves, impact force (GN), seismograph frequency (Hz), and distance.
